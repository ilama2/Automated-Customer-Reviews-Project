# Product Recommendations for Batteries

## Top 3 Recommended Products

### AmazonBasics AA Performance Alkaline Batteries (48 Count) - Packaging May Vary
**What customers love:** If you are looking for cheap non-rechargeable batteries that last quite a while then these are perfect. This was my second purchase of amazon batteries and they work great. Just as good or even better than name brand batteries and half the price.

**Common complaints:** Batteries fail in a short time on items like wireless thermometers and wall clocks. On an average changing is required within a month. They are cheaper but I'd rather pay a little bit more and have them last a lot longer.

### AmazonBasics AAA Performance Alkaline Batteries (36 Count)
**What customers love:** AmazonBasics AA AAA batteries have done well by me appear to have a good shelf life. Can't believe I didn't start buying them sooner! The packages are large and the price is great too.

**Common complaints:** Batteries don't hold the amount of high power juice like energizer or duracell, but they are half the price. My Christmas gifts the month of (december) only lasted like 2months toys now need replacement batteries.
