# Product Recommendations for Ebook readers

## Top 3 Recommended Products

### Kindle Voyage E-reader, 6 High-Resolution Display (300 ppi) with Adaptive Built-in Light, PagePress Sensors, Free 3G + Wi-Fi - Includes Special Offers
**What customers love:** For serious readers and users of library e-books, completly worth it. Never have to seek out light to read by (a noticeable improvement in the backlight) Great battery life... Great build quality... I expect it to last a long time.

**Common complaints:** Few or no complaints found for this product.

### Kindle Oasis E-reader with Leather Charging Cover - Black, 6" High-Resolution Display (300 ppi), Wi-Fi - Includes Special Offers
**What customers love:** Best Kindle ever made, best e-Reader on the market. Amazon still seems to be back-ordered. Wife love it, reads a lot and this is her go to reader. Great lightweight and convenient to use.

**Common complaints:** This is not an upgrade by any means! My three year old kindle outperformed Oasis. Magnetic connector is poorly designed and grows weak. Amazon Customer Services promises it will resolved in an update.

### Kindle Voyage E-reader, 6 High-Resolution Display (300 ppi) with Adaptive Built-in Light, PagePress Sensors, Wi-Fi - Includes Special Offers
**What customers love:** This is a great e-reader, perhaps a little pricey but well worth the money. A bit small, but very useful!! I bought this so i can save all of my books. Overall i am very satistfy a decent gift.

**Common complaints:** Amazon Voyage dead trying to update software My Kindle Voyage is nearly 16 months old and worked perfectly. I accidentally cracked my screen on the first day when the Voyage was too close to my rocking chair. Amazon offered $15 off purchasing the Paperwhite and that wasn't something I wanted at all.

## Key Differences Between Top Products

Based on customer reviews, these products differ in terms of durability, ease of use, and value for money.

## Product to Avoid

### Fire Tablet, 7 Display, Wi-Fi, 16 GB - Includes Special Offers, Black
**Why you should avoid it:** The security, Webroot Anywhere, that was included was impossible to install. The protector was not made for the Fire 7 as it was to large and had no directions that were coherent. The sound quality when I play music is terrible.
